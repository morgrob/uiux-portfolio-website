I’m Morgan

I’m a product designer who thrives in ambiguity, enjoys the challenge of defining the problem and finding the best solution, focuses on craft, and believes the devil is in the details.

Currently, I’m a UX Designer at Experian where I work on the consumer-facing experience for browsing and applying for personal loans. Before that, I designed for other companies including Intuit and Intel, and I taught UI/UX design fundamentals to college students through a nonprofit.

Design Superpowers

Emphasis on the Overlooked

I design for people and needs that are too often missed—from children with Cortical Visual Impairment to students navigating campus with limited mobility.

Chaos to Order

I turn unfamiliar, technical domains into clear experiences, whether that means data-pipeline workflows, athletic performance data, or personal finance.

Options, Options, Options

I explore broadly, test ideas with users and collaborators, and refine the strongest direction through feedback and thoughtful iteration.

Designing for the Problem

I ground decisions in research and evidence, focusing on the user’s underlying problem rather than jumping straight to a polished interface.
