My Design Tenets

Emphasis on the Overlooked

The best solutions always start with understanding the people actually experiencing the problem. I prioritize user perspectives throughout the design process and actively seek out edge cases to ensure that nothing slips through the cracks.

Designing for the Problem

Good design should adapt to people, not force people to adapt to it! I focus on understanding real behaviors to create solutions that work naturally within users' lives.

Chaos to Order

Whether it's organizing information, untangling complicated user flows, or cleaning up messy interfaces, I thrive on making things more intuitive and manageable.

Options, Options, Options

I aim to explore as many solutions as possible before committing to an approach. I work quickly and iteratively to produce a wide range of concepts, challenge assumptions, and find the strongest direction. I believe the most creative solutions come from this!
